About Me

Hey there! I'm Thomas J. Barton, and if it's got a screen or a circuit board, chances are I can fix it. While my day job is with The Home Depot Freight Team, my true passion lies in bringing tech back to life. From phone screen repairs to complex PC component swaps, I thrive on the challenge and the satisfaction of finding solutions that make your life easier.

My journey into tech began with web programming, sparking a curiosity that quickly evolved into a knack for troubleshooting and repair. Since High School, I've relentlessly honed my skills, learning everything from cloning hard drives and customizing Windows OS to confidently replacing phone and laptop components. This self-taught evolution has made me incredibly resourceful and adaptable—a true "Jack of All Trades" driven by an insatiable curiosity.

What drives me? Seeing the relief and happiness on people's faces when their stressed-out tech problems vanish. I'm a natural listener, genuinely caring, and I'm dedicated to being the trusted individual you can always turn to for reliable, affordable tech help.
